# Simul Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [DATE]

## The short version

Simul helps two people build habits together. We collect only what the app needs to work: your sign-in details, your profile, your habits and check-ins, and the photos you choose to attach. We don't run ads, don't sell your data, and don't track you across other apps or websites. Only the one other person in your household can see your habits and photos.

## What we collect and why

- **Account details.** Your email address if you sign in with an emailed code, or your Apple identifier (and email or Apple's private relay address, if you choose to share it) if you use Sign in with Apple. We use it to sign you in and keep your account.
- **Profile.** Your display name, avatar colour and, if you add one, a profile photo.
- **Habit data.** The habits you create (name, icon, schedule, time of day), your check-ins with their dates, and the streaks and achievements worked out from them.
- **Proof photos.** Photos you optionally attach to a check-in so your partner can approve it.
- **Notification settings.** Whether you allowed reminders on your device.
- **Technical data.** Our hosting provider processes standard technical data, such as your IP address, in server logs to run and secure the service.

## What we don't do

- No advertising and no selling or renting of personal data.
- No tracking across other apps or websites.
- No third-party analytics or advertising tools in the app.
- No access to your contacts or your location.

## Who sees your data

Simul is built for two people. The other person in your household can see your name, colour, avatar, shared habits, check-ins and proof photos. No other user can see them. We share data only with the service providers below, or where the law requires it.

## How long we keep it, and your choices

- We keep your data while your account exists.
- If you leave a household, your personal habits and check-ins are deleted.
- You can delete your account in the app (**Settings → Delete account**). This removes your account and your data from our systems.
- You can also ask us at **[CONTACT EMAIL]** to access, correct or delete your data. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA) you may also have the right to receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict certain processing, and complain to your local data protection authority. We will reply within one month.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to the database is restricted so you can only reach your own household's data. Your sign-in session is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

## International transfers

Your data is processed in the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for transfers from the EU, UK or elsewhere.

## Children

Simul is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.
